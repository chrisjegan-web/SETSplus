💡 Building an Effective Workout
Science-backed guidelines:
• Start with 1-2 compound exercises (multi-joint, most muscle activation)
• Add 2-3 isolation exercises (target specific muscles)
• Aim for 12-20 total sets per muscle group per week
• Balance push/pull exercises (1:1 ratio prevents imbalances)
• 3-5 exercises per workout is ideal for most people
Example balanced upper body:
1. Bench Press (compound chest) - 3 sets
2. Rows (compound back) - 3 sets
3. Cable Flyes (isolation chest) - 3 sets
4. Face Pulls (isolation rear delts) - 3 sets
5. Curls (arms) - 2 sets

Why this works:
• Balanced push/pull = injury prevention + shoulder health
• Compound first = maximum energy for heavy lifts
• Isolation after = target weak points & add volume
• 14 total sets = optimal growth stimulus without overtraining
